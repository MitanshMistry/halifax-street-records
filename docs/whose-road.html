Road responsibility

Whose road is this?

HRM handles reports for roads it maintains. For roads maintained by the Province, including 100-series highways, HRM directs residents to Nova Scotia 511.

Different roads are maintained by different governments. HRM points residents to its Winter – Street Information Map to check who maintains a road. This site does not include a map or ask for an address.

  1. Check who maintains the road

    Use HRM’s Winter – Street Information Map, linked from HRM’s pothole page, to check who maintains the road.

    Checked on 25 November 2026. HRM: Potholes and Sinkholes.

  2. If the Province maintains the road

    HRM directs residents to call 511 about provincial roads and 100-series highways.

    Checked on 25 November 2026. HRM: Potholes and Sinkholes.

  3. If HRM maintains the road

    Use HRM’s online pothole form or call 311. The form is on HRM’s website; this project does not collect or forward addresses.

    Checked on 25 November 2026. HRM: Potholes and Sinkholes.

  4. If you are unsure

    Check HRM’s map or call HRM at 311. Do not send an address to this site.

    Checked on 25 November 2026. HRM: 311 Municipal Services and Information.
