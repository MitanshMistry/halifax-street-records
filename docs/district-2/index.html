What do “half” and “9 in 10” mean?

For District 2 — Lawrencetown - The Lakes - Chezzetcook - Eastern Shore, “half” is the estimated time by which HRM had recorded a close date for about half of reports. “9 in 10” is the estimate for about nine in ten reports, when the data supports it. Reports without a close date are counted only up to the HRM data download date; they are not assumed closed. These past records cannot predict when your report will close.

HRM’s “Street Surface Maintenance” category covers more than potholes. This site shows past records; it cannot look up an individual report.

What this can't tell you

  • This shows when HRM's record was marked closed. The work may have been done earlier or later.

  • Reports show where people reported road surface problems, not where every problem is.
  • This page uses HRM data downloaded on 24 November 2026; it does not update live.
  • These HRM records do not cover roads maintained by the Province.
